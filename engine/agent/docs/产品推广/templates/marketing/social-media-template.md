## Platform-Specific Formats

### LinkedIn Post
```
[Compelling Hook]

[Main content - 2-3 paragraphs]

[Key insight or takeaway]

[CTA or question to encourage comments]

#Hashtags
```

**LinkedIn Guidelines**:
- Length: 1,300-3,000 characters (3-5 paragraphs)
- Format: Use line breaks and bullet points for readability
- Media: Include image, document, or video
- Engagement: Ask questions or share personal experiences
- Hashtags: Use 3-5 relevant hashtags
- Best time: 8-10 AM or 5-6 PM on weekdays

---

### Twitter/X Post
```
[Compelling message - max 280 characters]

[Link to content]

#Hashtags
```

**Twitter/X Guidelines**:
- Length: Max 280 characters (use thread for longer content)
- Media: Include image or GIF when possible
- Engagement: Polls work well for engagement
- Hashtags: Use 1-3 hashtags max
- Mentions: Tag relevant accounts
- Best time: 9 AM, 12 PM, or 3 PM on weekdays

---

### Facebook Post
```
[Compelling headline or question]

[Main content - engaging and conversational]

[Image or video]

[CTA]

#Hashtags
```

**Facebook Guidelines**:
- Length: 40-80 characters (posts shorter than 80 characters get more engagement)
- Media: Image posts get 2.3x more engagement
- Engagement: Ask questions to encourage comments
- Hashtags: Use 2-3 hashtags
- Best time: 1-4 PM on weekdays

---

### Instagram Post
```
[Image/video - focus on visual storytelling]

[Compelling caption - shorter for feed, longer for Stories]

[Emojis for engagement]

[Location tag]

[CTA in bio]

#Hashtags
```

**Instagram Guidelines**:
- Visual: High-quality, visually appealing content
- Caption: 138-150 characters optimal
- Stories: Use interactive features (polls, questions)
- Hashtags: Use 9-15 relevant hashtags
- Best time: 11 AM or 7 PM on weekdays

## Visual Guidelines

### Image Specifications
- **LinkedIn**: 1200x627 pixels (recommended)
- **Twitter/X**: 1200x675 pixels
- **Facebook**: 1200x630 pixels
- **Instagram**: 1080x1080 pixels (square) or 1080x1920 (vertical)

### Best Practices
- Use brand colors consistently
- Include logo when appropriate
- Use high-quality images (no blur or pixelation)
- Add text overlay for key message
- Consider mobile-first design
- Use infographics for data/statistics

## Engagement Strategy

### Post-Publishing Activities
1. **First Hour**: Monitor comments, respond promptly
2. **First 24 Hours**: Engage with commenters, share to stories
3. **First Week**: Re-share if getting good engagement
4. **Ongoing**: Pin top-performing posts

### Responding to Comments
- [ ] Thank users for positive comments
- [ ] Answer questions promptly and helpfully
- [ ] Address negative feedback professionally
- [ ] Ask follow-up questions to keep conversation going
- [ ] Share comments to stories (LinkedIn/Instagram)

### Cross-Promotion
- Share Twitter posts to LinkedIn (when appropriate)
- Repost user-generated content (with permission)
- Link to related blog posts or videos
- Tag collaborators or partners

## Testing & Optimization

### A/B Testing Elements
- Headlines/hook variations
- Image vs. video vs. text-only
- Post length (short vs. long)
- Posting times
- Hashtag combinations
- CTA language
- Media type (carousel vs. single image)

### Metrics to Track
- **Engagement Rate**: (likes + comments + shares) / followers
- **Reach/Impressions**: How many people saw the post
- **Click-Through Rate**: Link clicks / impressions
- **Share Rate**: Shares / impressions
- **Comment Rate**: Comments / impressions
- **Follower Growth**: New followers from post

### Analysis Process
1. Review performance after 24-48 hours
2. Identify top-performing posts
3. Analyze why they performed well
4. Note patterns in content, timing, format
5. Apply learnings to future posts
6. Document best practices
