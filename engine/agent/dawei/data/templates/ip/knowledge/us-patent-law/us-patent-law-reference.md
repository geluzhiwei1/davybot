# US Patent Law Knowledge Base (35 USC + 37 CFR + MPEP)

## Core Statutory Requirements

### 35 USC § 101 — Patent Eligibility
- Process, machine, manufacture, or composition of matter
- Alice/Mayo two-step test: (1) is it a patent-ineligible concept? (2) additional elements that transform it into patent-eligible application?
- Judicial exceptions: abstract ideas, laws of nature, natural phenomena

### 35 USC § 102 — Novelty
- AIA first-inventor-to-file system (effective March 16, 2013)
- Prior art: public disclosure before effective filing date
- Grace period: inventor's own disclosure within 1 year
- Secret prior art (102(e)): earlier US application by another

### 35 USC § 103 — Nonobviousness
- Graham factors: (1) scope and content of prior art, (2) differences, (3) level of ordinary skill
- Secondary considerations: commercial success, long-felt need, failure of others, unexpected results
- KSR: flexible approach to combining references, no rigid "teaching-suggestion-motivation" test

### 35 USC § 112 — Specification and Claims
- **§ 112(a)**: Written description, enablement, best mode
- **§ 112(b)**: Claims particularly pointing out and distinctly claiming the invention
- **§ 112(f)**: Means-plus-function — construed to cover corresponding structure in specification
- **§ 112(6)** (pre-AIA): Same as § 112(f)

## Patent Application Requirements (37 CFR)

### Specification (37 CFR 1.71-1.77)
- Title, cross-references, background, summary, brief description of drawings
- Detailed description: sufficient to enable one skilled in the art (enablement requirement)
- Sequence listings if applicable

### Claims (37 CFR 1.75)
- At least one claim required
- Independent claims: stand alone, define essential elements
- Dependent claims: refer to earlier claim, further limit
- Multiple dependent claims: refer to alternatives (special fee applies)
- Means-plus-function claims under § 112(f)

### Abstract (37 CFR 1.72)
- 150 words maximum
- Technical disclosure summary, not a claim scope definition

### Drawings (37 CFR 1.81-1.85)
- Required when necessary for understanding
- Specific formatting: margins, line weights, reference numerals
- Black and white only (color with petition)

## Patent Term
- Utility: 20 years from earliest effective US filing date
- Design: 15 years from grant date (35 USC § 173)
- Patent term adjustment (PTA) for USPTO delays (35 USC § 154(b))
- Terminal disclaimers for obviousness-type double patenting

## Provisional Applications (35 USC § 111(b))
- No formal claims required
- No oath/declaration required
- 12-month pendency, must file non-provisional to claim priority
- NOT examined — provides priority date only
- Enablement is critical for priority benefit

## Key Examination Concepts

### Restriction Requirement
- Unity of invention: "general inventive concept" (37 CFR 1.141)
- Election of species/groups required before examination

### Rejections and Responses
- § 101 rejections: subject matter eligibility
- § 102 rejections: anticipation (single reference)
- § 103 rejections: obviousness (may combine references)
- § 112 rejections: written description, enablement, definiteness
- After-final responses: require preappeal or RCE
